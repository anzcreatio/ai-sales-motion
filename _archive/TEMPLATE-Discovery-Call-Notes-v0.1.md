# Discovery call notes: template v0.1 (generic, all industries)

> **How to use**
> 1. **Before the call (Claude, 10 min):** fill Part A from the lead handoff and account research. The AE and SC check it.
> 2. **During the call (AE takes notes; the SC leads the questions):** tick, circle and quote. Don't write prose. Write the customer's **exact words in "quotes"**. They become the demo labels and the Proposal's "what we heard".
> 3. **Within 1 hour of the call:** give these notes (and the transcript, if recorded) to Claude. Claude produces the Discovery Brief, the qualification score and the "What we heard" one-pager.
> 4. If the call isn't recorded, these notes are the **only** record. Fill sections 1, 4, 6 and 8 at minimum.
>
> Legend: ✔ = they confirmed · ✘ = not true for them · ? = unclear or not asked · **S** = they said it · **H** = hinted or implied

## Part C: produced by Claude after the call (not filled by hand)

1. **Discovery Brief**: every field tagged Confirmed / Inferred / Assumed.
2. **Qualification score + demo go/no-go**: Green (demo) · Amber (demo only if the economic buyer or an influencer attends) · Red (no demo; these 2–3 questions first). **The SC signs it.**
3. **"What we heard" one-pager** for the customer (sent within 24 h): journey picture with their pain points, the outcome we'll show, 3–5 yes/no checks, demo agenda + attendees.
4. **Gaps list**: what the demo will treat as Assumed and show as validation moments.
