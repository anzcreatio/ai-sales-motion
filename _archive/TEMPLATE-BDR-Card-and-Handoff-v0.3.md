# BDR call sheet v0.3

**Your job:** find a real pain and book 30 minutes with our solution team. **Ask, listen, don't pitch.**
The call is recorded, so you don't take notes: Claude reads the transcript.

---

**0. Start:** "Mind if I record this so I don't miss anything?"

**1. Open**

*Cold call:*
> "Most [industry] leaders we talk to say one of three things keeps coming up:
> **one**, getting the full picture of a customer means asking three different teams;
> **two**, the business moves faster than the systems, so a new product or rule takes months to go live;
> **three**, everyone's being asked to do something with AI, but most are still working out where it pays off,
> or their agents never got past the pilot.
> **Which of those is closest to home for you?**"

*They contacted us:* "Thanks for reaching out. **What prompted it?**"

**2. Then ask, in this order**

| # | Ask |
|---|---|
| 1 | "Where does that get hard today?" |
| 2 | "How do you handle it now? What do you use?" |
| 3 | "What happens if nothing changes?" |
| 4 | "Is fixing it on anyone's plan this year? Why now?" |
| 5 | "Who else cares about this, and who would decide?" |
| 6 | **AI only:** "Which job would you want AI to take on first?" |

**3. Book**, but only if you heard ✔ a pain in their words ✔ who owns it ✔ a timeframe:
> "Let's get 30 minutes with our solution team. Who should be there, and when suits?"

Not all three? Send a thank-you email and leave it in nurture.

---

| If they… | Say |
|---|---|
| say "all three" | "If you could fix only one, which would it be?" |
| say "none" | "What's taking up most of your team's time right now?" |
| go deep or ask "can it do X?" | "That's exactly what our specialist will dig into. Let's get them in the room." |
| ask about price | "That depends on what we'd solve. The session will show you that first." |

**Never:** pitch, demo, quote prices, promise what it will do, or criticise their current tools.

**After the call:** save the recording or transcript to the deal and tell the AE the booked time.
Not recorded? Write 3 lines: their pain in their words · who's coming · when.

---

*For AEs and SCs, not BDRs:* the SC runs `workflows/FM-First-Meeting-Prep.md` on the transcript: it extracts the six
answers, then builds the playback and solution-hypothesis slides and the light-config brief. Track: pain one → CRM ·
two → Business Studio (no-code apps) · three or question 6 → AI Studio.
