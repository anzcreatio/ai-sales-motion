# BDR call card + handoff form: template v0.1 (generic, all industries)

> **Your job on this call:** earn a 30-minute session with our solution team. **Don't run discovery.**
> Ask the 5 questions (about 3 minutes), capture their words, book the meeting, fill in the handoff form.
> When they start explaining a problem in detail, write down **one sentence in their exact words** and park it.

---

## Part 1: the call card

### Step 1: the opening question (everyone)

**Q1. "What made you interested in this right now?"**

Their answer decides the branch:
- They talk about **customers, sales, service, their current CRM, or cost/licences** → **CRM branch**
- They talk about **AI, agents, automating tasks, chatbots, copilots** → **AI Studio branch**
- Both → ask CRM 2 and AI 2, then carry on

AI variant of Q1, if they were contacted about AI: *"What's the first thing you'd want AI to do for your team?"*

### Step 2: branch questions (2 of them)

| CRM branch | AI Studio branch |
|---|---|
| **CRM 2.** "Where would it help most: winning customers, serving them, or running operations?" | **AI 2.** "What's the first task you'd want AI to take off your team's plate?" |
| **CRM 3.** "What are you using today, and roughly how many people use it?" | **AI 3.** "Where does that work happen today: which systems, email, phone, documents?" |
| | **AI 3b.** "Is there an AI policy or team that would need to be involved?" |

### Step 3: closing questions (everyone)

**Q4. "Is anything driving the timing: a renewal, a project, a deadline?"**

**Q5. "Who else would want to be part of the conversation?"**

Then book: *"Let's get 30 minutes with our solution team. They'll come prepared with how organisations like yours
handle this. Does [day/time] work for you and [names from Q5]?"*

### Parking lines (when they go deep)

- "That's exactly what our specialist will want to dig into. Let me note it so they come prepared."
- "Great question. I'd rather get you a proper answer than a quick one. That's why I'd like to set up 30 minutes with our solution team."
- "I won't do it justice on this call. Can I write down what you just said, word for word, so we start there?"

### Don't

- ✘ Pitch features, demo, or talk about pricing
- ✘ Answer "can it do X?" (park it: "Great question for the session")
- ✘ Ask about their process step by step, volumes or KPIs
- ✘ Promise what the solution will do

### Booking threshold

| Book the discovery if… | Otherwise |
|---|---|
| **CRM:** interest area + a trigger + at least one business-side person attending | Nurture |
| **AI Studio:** a **named task** + a business owner | "Exploring AI" with no task → nurture, or an AI ideas workshop (target accounts only, ask the SC) |
| IT-only audience or "just send me info" | Nurture |
