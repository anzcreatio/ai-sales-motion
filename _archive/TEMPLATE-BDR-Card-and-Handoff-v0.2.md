# BDR / AE call card + handoff form: template v0.2 (all industries)

> **Your job on this call:** find out whether there's a pain worth a session with our solution team, and if there
> is, book it. **About 5–7 minutes of questions. Mostly listening.**
> Write down their **exact words**. When they go deep, park it (Part 1, step 6).

---

## Part 1: the call card

### Step 1: open (pick by lead type)

**Cold outreach**, read this, then **stop talking**:

> "Most [industry] leaders we talk to say one of three things keeps coming up.
>
> **One:** to see the full picture of a customer, someone has to ask three different teams.
>
> **Two:** the business moves faster than the systems. A new product or rule takes months to go live, so people work
> around the system in the meantime.
>
> **Three:** everyone's being asked to do something with AI. Most teams are either still working out where it
> actually pays off, or they've built a few agents that never got past the pilot.
>
> Which of those is closest to home for you?"

- **"All three"** → "If you could fix only one, which would it be?"
- **"None"** → "What's taking up most of your team's time right now?" Nothing real → close politely, nurture.
- Short version (executives, little time): *"Most leaders we talk to mention one of three things: no single view of the
  customer, systems that can't keep up with change, or AI that hasn't got past the pilot. Which is closest to home?"*

**Raised hand** (they contacted us), skip the three pains:

> "Thanks for reaching out. What prompted it?"

Their answer gives you the track. Go to step 3.

### Step 2: the follow-up for their pick (cold only)

| They picked | Ask | Track |
|---|---|---|
| **One:** full picture of the customer | "When you need the full story on a customer, who do you have to ask, and how long does it take?" | **CRM** |
| **Two:** systems can't keep up | "What was the last change the business needed, and how long did it take to go live?" | **No-code** |
| **Three:** AI | "Where are you with it: still working out where it fits, or already building?" (then the AI row below) | **AI agents** |

AI follow-ups, by answer:

| They say | Ask next |
|---|---|
| "Still working it out" | "If AI could take one job off your team tomorrow, which would it be?" |
| "We've built some agents" | "What's stopping them from going live: the data, access to your systems, or sign-off from risk?" |
| "We use Copilot / ChatGPT" | "Is it doing the work, or helping people write about the work?" |
| "We're fine" | "What would have to be true for AI to be worth a second look next year?" → nurture |

### Step 3: five core questions (everyone, every call)

1. **"Where does that get hard today?"**
2. **"How do you handle it now: which systems, spreadsheets or people?"**
3. **"What happens if it stays as it is?"**
4. **"Is fixing this on anyone's plan for the next 6–12 months? What's driving the timing?"**
5. **"Who else would care about fixing this, and who would decide?"**

### Step 4: track add-on (ask the column for their track)

| CRM | No-code apps | AI agents |
|---|---|---|
| "Where would it help most: winning customers, serving them, or running operations?" | "Which process or app is the problem?" | "Which task should the agent do?" |
| "What do you use today?" | "Who builds and changes it today, and how long does a change take?" | "Where does that work and its data live today?" |
| "Roughly how many people use it?" | "How many apps or tools like that do you have?" | "Is there an AI policy, and who approves AI?" |

Both CRM and AI (or any mix): ask the first question of each column.

### Step 5: book, or not

**Book the session with our solution team only if all three are true:**
- ☐ A **pain in their own words** (not just "interested")
- ☐ **Someone who owns that pain** will attend
- ☐ **Some timeframe**, even "this financial year"

AI agents also need **a named task** ("look at AI" isn't one).

Book it: *"Let's get 30 minutes with our solution team. They'll come prepared with how organisations like yours handle
this. Does [day/time] work for you and [names from Q5]?"*

Otherwise: one useful follow-up email, then nurture. **No solution-team time.** IT-only audience or "just send me
info" → nurture.

### Step 6: parking lines (when they go deep)
- "That's exactly what our specialist will want to dig into. Let me note it so they come prepared."
- "Great question. I'd rather get you a proper answer than a quick one. That's what the session is for."
- "Can I write down what you just said, word for word, so we start there next time?"

### Don't
- ✘ Pitch features, demo, or talk about pricing
- ✘ Answer "can it do X?" (park it)
- ✘ Promise what the solution will do
- ✘ Ask "what system do you use?" before you know the pain (core questions first, track questions second)
- ✘ Criticise their current tools or vendors
