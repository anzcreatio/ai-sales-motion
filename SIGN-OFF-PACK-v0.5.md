## 1. The change in one page

| | Today | Pilot motion |
|---|---|---|
| Customer meetings before a proposal | 3–4 (AE call, SC call, demo, follow-ups) | **2**: a 30-min discovery + the demo |
| How we learn about the customer | Open questions; the customer explains | **Our homework first**: AI research + industry packs → hypotheses the customer confirms or corrects |
| Who builds the demo | An SE, via a script and a review call | **SC + Claude**, from reusable modules; SE only for escalations |
| Which deals get a demo | Most | **Qualified first**: 🟢 full demo · 🟠 1-day reference demo · 🔴 video only |
| What the demo shows | Features | Their pain, their terms, their KPI; **D1 + D3 + D2/D5** in every demo |
| Proposal and partner handover | Written by hand, if at all | **Generated from one source** within 48 h; checked against each other |
| Consistency across 8 AEs and 3 SCs | Depends on the person | Same cards, workflows, packs and gates for everyone |

**Goal:** a higher win rate, through better qualification, sharper demos and faster follow-up.

## 5. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| AEs route around the go/no-go gate | Sales Director sponsorship; the 🟠 tier still gives them a demo |
| Pre-call packs carry a wrong fact into the call | SC check is mandatory; numbers kept in exact units |
| AI governance questions kill BFSI deals late | Governance pack lite sent after discovery; never say what isn't confirmed |
| Token cost of W1 with 8 AEs | Once per booked discovery, not per lead; measured in the pilot |
| Too much process for 3 SCs | Gates are checklists, not meetings; review at week 6 and cut what didn't earn its place |
| Proposal promises the partner can't deliver | Partner Pack "commitments made in the sale"; the partner confirms in writing |

## 6. Pilot plan

- **Scope:** 3 SCs, the next 4–6 new opportunities, at least one each of BFSI, Manufacturing and General; at least one AI Studio-led.
- **Before day 1:** baseline from the last ~10 deals (discovery → demo days, meetings before proposal, win rate if known).
- **Measure:** see process §13. **Review:** week 6 → v1.0, then decide W3, the master sandbox build and wider rollout.
