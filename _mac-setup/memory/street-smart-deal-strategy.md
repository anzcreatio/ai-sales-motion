When Renzo asks for deal strategy or competitive research, give a practical read of how the decision will really get made, not "hypotheses" or academic frameworks. Cover: why the meeting really exists (relationship, leverage, favour), each stakeholder's real want and fear, who can quietly block it, how the culture decides, budget timing, and the "tell" that checks each read, then concrete moves.

**Why:** Yokogawa, 20 Nov 2026: "we don't come up with theoretical ideas … I don't want scientific hypothesis, i want street smart thinking." Money and technology matter, but politics and people decide.

**How to apply:** Write the deal read as tables: reasons with tells, people with want/fear/how to win, killers with counters, the play. Evidence tags still apply. Related: [[challenge-me]], [[ai-sales-motion-redesign]].
