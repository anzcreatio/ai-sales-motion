On customer-facing architecture slides, Renzo cuts boxes and arrows down to short labels in plain business words: no sublines, qualifiers, approvals or "how it works" detail (BAF, 17 Nov 2026: gateway subline removed, governance shown as labels only, "Scores" → "Results").

**Why:** the slide is presented and talked through; detail on the slide reads as clutter and invites questions the SC can't yet answer.

**How to apply:** draft customer diagrams at label level from the start. Put open checks, sources and caveats in an internal page or chat, not on the slide, and keep the .pptx speaker notes customer-safe (internal draft notes leak if the file is sent). See [[challenge-me]] for flagging claims like a named LLM provider against the governance pack.
