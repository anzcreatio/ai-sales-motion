Never write a specific calendar date (e.g. "Early December", "by mid-December", "Fri 4 Dec") on a customer deck's next-steps slide or in the agenda's "Today's goal", unless the customer has already agreed that date. The slide shows the steps only. Timing and owners go in the speaker notes, to agree in the room.

**Why:** Renzo, 24 Nov 2026 (Yokogawa): "stop putting specific dates because sometimes it will not be accurate". He stripped the dates, the session length, the partner and the region from the generated next steps.

**How to apply:** DECK-STANDARD v0.7 + the builder's warning enforce it. Also keep dates out of the closing question. Related: [[challenge-me]], [[customer-slides-labels-not-explanations]].
