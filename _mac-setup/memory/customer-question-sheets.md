Customer question sheets (RFP clarifications etc.) come in two versions. The **working** xlsx for the SC has topic, "what would help us", "why we're asking" and status columns. The **customer** xlsx has only # · Criterion IDs · Question · Answer.

Customer questions: short; the reason as one sentence inside the question, only where it helps; state the assumption we'll use if unanswered; cut what we can assume or what shows our hand; offer to answer optional sections instead of opting out; no references to earlier calls or promises.

**Why:** Renzo edited the Navalo clarification sheet on 19 Nov 2026. He used the extra columns to edit, then deleted them and cut 7 questions for the version Navalo sees. Feedback file: SharePoint `Lessons/feedback/2026-11-19-renzo-huruiti-rfp-clarification-sheet.md`.

**How to apply:** For any question list to a customer, build both files from the start. Draft in his voice (see [[questions-first]] for chat; customer files follow WRITING-STYLE). Never put internal "why" text in the customer file.
