On every new deal, ask the SC at the top first: "introduction to Creatio, or a detailed demo on their specific pains?"
Introduction → current plan (light config). Detailed → best-practice pains from the pack (tag I), success-criteria
email so the customer ranks them, Full demo plan via sc-demo-plan; guide the SC through it.

**Why:** Renzo, 13 Nov 2026: AE/BDR bookings sometimes set a higher bar than an intro, and customers often don't
state their pains because they expect us to know best practice from our industry position.

**How to apply:** sc-first-meeting step 0b + rule 7b; sc-guide mode B new-deal row. Related: [[questions-first]], [[build-plan-before-build]], [[agenda-no-questions-first]].
