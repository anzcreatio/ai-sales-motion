Don't put a "your questions first: what would make this a success?" item on a customer agenda slide (Renzo, 12 Nov 2026).

**Why:** if the demo we built doesn't match the success they describe in the room, we've set ourselves up to miss in front of them.

**How to apply:** agenda = intro + what we'd like to agree, recap ("did we get it right?"), demo, the decision question, next steps. Get success criteria before the meeting (pre-call, AE email, discovery), not live. Applies to the `agenda` slide type in sc-deck and any AE brief. Related: [[ai-sales-motion-redesign]].
