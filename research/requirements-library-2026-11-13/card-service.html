I'd start with

  1. Emails to our service addresses become cases automatically, with the customer identified and an acknowledgement sent. standard
  2. Agents see the customer's details, products, open cases and recent contacts on one screen. standard
  3. Each case is routed to the right team by its category and priority, from rules the service team maintains. standard

Resolution and knowledge · 9 lines

Resolve first time

standardstart here
Agents find the right answer in a shared knowledge base without leaving the case.
Knowledge is hard to find, so answers vary and cases get escalated.
Time spent searching for information: ~19% of a knowledge worker's time (source: CORE pack v0.2 §4, McKinsey 2012, Medium, old; label it) · Show: standard: knowledge base linked to cases
standard
Agents reply using approved templates that fill in the customer's details.
Agents write the same replies from scratch, and quality varies.
Show: standard: email templates on the case
standard
AI suggests a reply drawn from the knowledge base, which the agent edits before sending.
Agents spend time finding and rewording policy answers.
