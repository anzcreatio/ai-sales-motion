# Creatio Differentiator Catalogue: Claim Verification Research

Researched 3 Nov 2026 for the APAC presales team. About 20 web searches plus direct page reads.

**How to read this:**
- "Vendor" means the source is the company making the claim about itself. It is marketing, not independent proof.
- "3rd-party blog" means a consultancy or aggregator site. Treat its figures as indicative only.
- Quotes are kept under 15 words.
- Where a figure appears only in a search-result summary and I did not read the page myself, it is marked *(search snippet, not page-read)*.

**Answer to "Is AI Studio sold separately?"** Since 1 May 2026, AI Studio is a separate *product name*, but it is **required by default with Business Studio licences** (A4). AI usage is metered through annual **AI credit packages** (B3). So: "AI Studio is part of every Business Studio licence; AI consumption is purchased as credit packages." Do not say "AI is free" or "AI is included at no extra cost". That was true of older pricing ("Creatio AI is included in all platform plans") but is now contradicted by the credit packages.
