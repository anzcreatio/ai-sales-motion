# Core pack: cross-industry KPI benchmark ranges

Researched 3 Nov 2026. Purpose: hypothesis-forming before a 20-minute discovery call ("is yours closer to X, Y or Z?"). These are not targets and not proof points for a business case.

**Source-quality flags**
- **Strong:** independent research, a peer-reviewed study, an industry body, or a regulator.
- **Medium:** an analyst firm press release or summary, a reputable trade press report, or a large vendor survey with a stated method (always marked "vendor").
- **Weak:** a vendor blog, a stats aggregator, or a single anecdote.

"Secondary" means I could not open the primary document and took the figure from a page that cites it.

## Use with care

1. **Use these numbers as conversation prompts, not proof.** Phrase them as "some organisations see X, others Y; where are you?". Never put a Weak figure on a slide as a benchmark.
2. **Vendor-authored data.** Salesforce (rep time), MuleSoft (IT backlog), Fenergo (KYC), Signicat (abandonment), Ebsta (win rate, cycle) and every containment figure come from companies that sell the fix. Salesforce is also a direct Creatio competitor, so citing its "State of" reports in front of a prospect can backfire. Prefer SQM, Gartner, HBR and ACXPA where possible.
3. **Definitions drive the number.** AHT with or without ACW, FCR measured by survey or by system, containment vs resolution, and "abandoned at least once" (Signicat) vs per-application drop-off can each move a figure by 2x. Ask the customer how they measure before comparing.
4. **Age and geography.** The HBR lead-response study (2011), McKinsey's 19% (2012) and CEB effort research are old and US-centric. APAC-specific data is thin: only ACXPA (Australian AHT about 537 s, wait time 1:20) and Ozonetel (India ACW) were found. Treat North American figures as approximate for APAC.
5. **Sector skew.** Sales-cycle and win-rate data (Ebsta/Pavilion) skew towards SaaS. Banking onboarding is measured in weeks to months for corporates and minutes to days for retail. Manufacturing quote cycles depend on engineering review. Adjust before quoting to a banking or manufacturing prospect.
6. **Gaps to fill with the team's own deal data.** These have no credible public source: B2C onboarding time and cost, NIGO / first-time-right rate, case resolution vs SLA, re-keying time, time to change business systems, and an absolute B2B win rate.
