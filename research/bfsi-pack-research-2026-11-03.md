# BFSI pack research: raw findings (3 Nov 2026)

Feeds [BFSI-PACK-v0.1.md](../packs/BFSI-PACK-v0.1.md). Research pass: **25 web searches + 8 page fetches** (3 blocked:
AFCA 403, KWM 403, ASIC IDR page 404; 2 PDFs read locally after fetch: the ASIC banking supervisory letter and the MFAA
release). Public information only.

**Quality scale:** **Strong** = regulator / legislation / industry body with stated data, read at or near the primary
source · **Medium** = reputable secondary (law firm, trade press reporting named data) or older primary data ·
**Weak** = vendor marketing, a single uncited figure, or a consumer guide · **Our estimate** = mock or experience.
**Read** = we read the page/PDF itself. **Summary** = the figure comes from a search-result summary only; check the page
before quoting.
