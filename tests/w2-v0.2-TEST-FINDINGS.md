Two blind runs of W2 v0.2 by separate Claude agents. Each saw only the workflow, the reference files and the call
inputs (not the earlier v0.1 Suntory output, not PRC's post-call solution hypothesis).

| Run | Inputs | Mode | Result | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [01 Suntory Oceania](w2-v0.2-01-suntory-oceania/02-W2-output.md) | Transcript only (intro pitch, no W1, no card) | Short, low confidence | P Amber, A Amber | 975 words in total (v0.1 run: ~3,300 across brief + one-pager) |
| [02 Perth Radiological Clinic](w2-v0.2-02-perth-radiological-clinic/02-W2-output.md) | Transcript + W1 pack (no card, no timestamps) | Short, low confidence | A **Red** (option: AI ideas workshop) | 600 internal + Red email |

## What worked
- Same colours as the v0.1 Suntory run, with about a third of the words. Output 0 (7 lines each) is readable in a minute.
- No assumed journey breaks or "from ? to" in customer text.
- The claims check is the most useful new part: 6 on Suntory, 12 on PRC, incl. "AI toolkit… we're not charging you for
  it", which breaks the catalogue's D7 "never say AI is free" line.

## What broke → fixed in W2 v0.2 (same version, before release)
| # | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The 600-word Short cap squeezed out things that matter: the Suntory one-pager **dropped the "10 questions" ask** and the "we don't use Salesforce" correction | Short cap 800; claims table outside the cap; "never cut" list (commitments, promises, positioning corrections), carried into the one-pager |
| 2 | Claims had no home; "one line each" in Output 0 broke the 15-line limit | New Output 2b claims table; Output 0 shows the count + worst two |
| 3 | Rule 7 (no empty rows) clashed with gap lists, whose job is to name what's missing | Exception for Output 0 item 4 and Output 4 |
| 4 | "What we'll show you" limited to Confirmed items made no sense for our own proposal | Findings stay Confirmed-only; the proposal must trace to a pain or idea they raised |
| 5 | Amber vs Red unclear when a pain is vague and has no consequence | Tie-break: stated pain + demo booked = Amber at worst; Red needs a §5.3 trigger with evidence |
| 6 | Unclear whether a customer-floated AI idea is a "named task" | Named = raised or agreed as theirs, with a trigger; a category ("the OCR one") is weak |
| 7 | Red template clashed with PRC: promised items (diagram, licence answer) and "let it sit a month" vs a day-7 nudge | Promised items go in the email; the nudge follows their timing |
| 8 | No timestamps (PRC); garbled speech-to-text quotes | "~40%" positions; keep garbled quotes with a bracketed reading, tagged I, never in customer text |
| 9 | Word counting undefined; quotes repeated in Outputs 0, 1, 2 | Counting rule; quote once in the Brief |
