## Output 7: Differentiators and competitor angle

| D-ID | Why it should land with this account | Proof moment we'd show |
|---|---|---|
| **D3** AI that acts, governed | Board wants AI; ASIC is reviewing customer-facing AI; contact centre lead believes in a human at the end | Agent answers a repayment question, blocks a card, then **asks for confirmation** before reissue; hardship flag → hands to a person with a summary |
| **D5** Fits your landscape | "Does it work with Microsoft?"; NiCE, Databricks and core banking already in place | Architecture slide: Creatio beside NiCE, M365, core and Databricks; simulated core sync |
| **D2** One platform | "Older CRM plus core screens"; service, case, hardship and AI on one record | Follow one customer from chat → case → hardship hand-off (sets up H5) |

**Competitor angle (one line each)**
- **NiCE (incumbent):** "Your AI lives in the contact centre." → We don't replace it: we're the service record and the agent that *acts* across channels, which NiCE hands off to.
- **Microsoft (Dynamics 365 + Copilot):** "You're already Microsoft." → We work with M365 and Azure-hosted AI; business teams change the process in hours, not projects.
- **Salesforce / ServiceNow:** "Enterprise service platform with agents." → Same outcome at customer-owned-bank scale and cost, faster to value (D6 if pushed).
- **In-house (data & AI team on Databricks):** "We'll build agents ourselves." → Use their data platform; we supply the governed workflow, case and front-line UI they'd otherwise build.

**Words to avoid / what to call it**
- Avoid **"replace your contact centre"**, **"chatbot"**, **"reduce headcount"**: the incumbent is a strategic partner and the lead values people. Say **"AI service agents that hand off to your people"**.
- Avoid leading with **"CRM"** (their CRM is "dated"; it invites a rip-and-replace debate). Say **"customer service workspace"**.
- Say **"customers"**, not "members" (the bank's own word). Don't raise the merger.
