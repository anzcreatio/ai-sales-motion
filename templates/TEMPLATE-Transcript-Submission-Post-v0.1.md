**📞 Had a discovery or BDR call? Drop the transcript here. Takes 30 seconds.**

1. In the Teams meeting chat, open **Recap → Transcript → Download** (.docx)
2. Drag the file into this channel
3. Done. The SC team gets a first-meeting pack drafted automatically and picks it up from there

**Please do it within 24 hours of the call.** No transcript, no SC on the next meeting.

Tip: turn on **Transcribe** at the start of the call (or set it to auto in Meeting options) or there's nothing to download.
Not a Teams call? Paste your notes into a Word doc and drop that in instead.
