# Partner Handover Pack: template v0.1

> **What this is:** the presales handover from Creatio to the implementation partner, **before contract**. It gives the
> partner enough to run its own discovery and requirements, and to estimate with stated confidence.
> **What it is not:** a specification. Nothing here is a requirement until the partner's discovery confirms it.
> Generated by W5 from the Discovery Brief, the demo build log, the demo recording and the Proposal; curated by the SC.
> Target length: ≤ 5,000 words (~10 pages) before appendices; if longer, keep the top 10 assumptions in §6 and move the full register to an appendix. Every item carries an evidence tag and, where relevant, a demo status.

**Legend:** **C** Confirmed by the customer (quote/date) · **I** Inferred from research · **A** Assumed (Industry Pack default)
**Demo status:** *Built* (works in the reference demo) · *Simulated* (looks real, isn't: e.g. a fake integration) · *Not shown*
**Buildability:** 🟢 OOTB/no-code pattern · 🟠 configuration with care · 🔴 code or real integration (partner/SE sign-off needed)
